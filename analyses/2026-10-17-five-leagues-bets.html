How to read the numbers

  • The bar is my estimated chance. The black tick is the break-even chance at the odds (1 divided by the odds). If the bar ends before the tick, the price is shorter than my estimate.
  • "Close to fair" means my estimate is within 3 points of break-even. "Priced short" means it is lower. "Above the price" means it is 3 or more points higher. Bookmaker prices include a margin, so most rows are priced short.
  • Only results and the quoted goals line have a price. Rows with no odds are estimates only.
  • "Model goals" are the goals each team is expected to score. They come from 70% bookmaker prices and 30% season scoring and conceding rates, so a match with no price leans fully on the season rates. Win and draw chances use the same 70/30 blend directly.

Limits

  • Teams have played between 5 and 9 league games, so season rates are noisy. Treat gaps of a few points as noise.
  • The goals model treats the two teams' goals as independent. It tends to understate draws and 0-0 or 1-1 results a little.
  • Combining several events into one bet multiplies the risk, and no result is guaranteed. Only stake money you can afford to lose.
