How to read this

  • The score is my single most likely scoreline, not a certainty. Football scores are spread widely, and the exact score is rarely above 15% likely.
  • Confidence is my judgment of how well the pick is supported by the data: Medium means a clear gap in the table plus consistent team news; Low means a close match, thin data or heavy absences.
  • The positions and points next to each team are the league table before today's games.

Limits

  • No odds were used: this is not a value analysis and not betting advice. No result is guaranteed. Only stake money you can afford to lose.

  • Numbers other than the table (goals scored and conceded) are from the same table and have not been checked against a second source.
