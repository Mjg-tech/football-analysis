How to read the numbers

  • The bar is my estimated chance. The black tick is the break-even chance at the odds (1 divided by the odds). If the bar ends before the tick, the price is shorter than my estimate.
  • "Close to fair" means my estimate is within 3 points of break-even. "Priced short" means it is lower. I found no event where the price clearly beats my estimate, because bookmaker prices include a margin.
  • Rows with no odds are estimates only. I could not check what a bookmaker would charge for them.
  • Estimates are rounded and rest on 7 games of data per team, so treat a gap of a few points as noise.

Method and limits

  • Goal events use each side's expected goals (from scoring and conceding rates this season and in the last 10 league games) and a standard goals model, then I blended in the market prices.
  • Corner estimates use each team's average total corners per game, which are noisy. Line-ups are not confirmed.
  • Sources disagree on some injuries and on Ajax's suspension (one list has Gaaei suspended, one lists him in the line-up). Check line-ups an hour before kick-off.
  • Combining several events into one bet multiplies the risk. Only stake money you can afford to lose, and be aware that no result is guaranteed.
